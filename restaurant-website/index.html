Our Story

A Passion for
Exceptional Dining

Founded in 2010, Maison Élan was born from a singular vision — to create a space where the finest ingredients meet masterful technique and warm hospitality. Nestled in the heart of Bhubaneswar, our restaurant has become a destination for those who believe that dining is an art form.

Our kitchen celebrates both classical French culinary tradition and the vibrant flavors of modern Indian cuisine — a harmonious marriage of East and West that surprises and delights with every visit.

The Masterminds

Meet Our Chefs

World-class culinary artists who transform the finest ingredients
into unforgettable dining experiences.

Aryan Mehta

Executive Chef

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris and with 18 years crafting culinary masterpieces across 3 continents, Chef Aryan leads Maison Élan's creative vision.

Sofia Moreau

Head Pastry Chef

A graduate of École Ferrandi Paris, Chef Sofia brings French patisserie artistry to Maison Élan, creating desserts that are as beautiful as they are divine.

Rohan Das

Sous Chef

An IHM Delhi alumnus who spent years in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, Chef Rohan brings innovative Indo-European fusion to every plate.
